## Options

- Before settling, lay out two or three genuinely different approaches, not variations of one. Include "extend what already exists" whenever that is possible.
- For each, state what it costs and what it buys: complexity, risk, fit with existing patterns, effort, and what it makes easier or harder later.
- Choose one and say why in a sentence. Record all of them under `options`, marking the chosen one.
- If one option clearly dominates, say so briefly instead of inventing weak alternatives.
